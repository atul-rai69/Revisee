# Revisee brand essentials

## Identity

- Product name: **Revisee**
- Tagline: **Learn. Revise. Remember.**
- Visual direction: warm-light **Living Study Notes**
- Compact mark: official `R` letterform with teal sparkles

## Core colors

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Logo navy | `#071638` |
| Logo teal | `#12BDAE` |
| Canvas | `#F5F2E8` |
| Paper | `#FFFCF5` |
| Interface ink | `#1D2928` |
| Primary action | `#147D73` |
| Sunrise amber | `#E9AD43` |
| Sunrise coral | `#DF765E` |

Amber and coral are supporting illustration accents, not replacements for the official teal logo accent.

## Typography

- Interface: Inter Variable, weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.
- Long-form theory and explanations: Source Serif 4 Variable, weights 400, 600 and 700.
- Self-host WOFF2 files in production.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- Keep clear space around the primary logo equal to the width of the lowercase `i` stem in the wordmark.
- Minimum primary-logo width: 120px digital, 32mm print.
- Minimum wordmark width: 100px digital.
- Minimum compact-mark size: 24px digital.
- Use `favicon.svg` below 24px rather than shrinking the detailed compact mark.

## Correct use

- Use the primary logo on canvas, paper and white surfaces.
- Use the light logo on navy or other sufficiently dark solid surfaces.
- Use monochrome artwork only where color reproduction is unavailable.
- Keep the original proportions and path geometry.
- Do not recolor the teal sparkles with sunrise accents.
- Do not add shadows, outlines, gradients, rotation or stretching to the logo.
- Do not place the logo over visually busy imagery without a solid backing surface.

## Motion

The core wordmark remains static. On login, onboarding or an intentional loader, the sparkles may enter with a restrained opacity/scale animation. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and do not run continuous logo animation in navigation.
